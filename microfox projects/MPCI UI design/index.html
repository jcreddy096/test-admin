Equivalent to a master’s degree, this 100% online programme comprises 6 modules and covers 180 credits. Each 12-week module features weekly video lessons and monthly group calls.

Flexible Pathways

Customise your experience with options to complete the full diploma, study individual modules, or focus on topping-up mentorship hours that fulfil your CMPC certification requirements.

Expert-Led Training

Global Accreditation

Why Professionals Choose The Mental Performance Coaching Institute.

We equip practitioners with the skills to impact individuals, groups, teams and organisations in creating psychologically informed environments. As a globally recognised institution based in Ireland, we are committed to delivering cutting-edge education led by applied practitioners with elite expertise.

Dr. David McHugh leads MPCI with a wealth of expertise in sport psychology and elite performance. Holding a Professional Doctorate, MSc in Sports Psychology, PgDip in Psychology, and a BA in Psychology and Geography, David combines academic excellence with over a decade of applied experience. He has worked with renowned organisations such as Washington Spirit, FAI, UEFA as an A Elite Coach, and GAA, bridging theory and practice to develop impactful programmes. His commitment ensures MPCI equips professionals with the mentorship and tools needed to excel in sport, business, and beyond.
